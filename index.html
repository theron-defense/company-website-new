Mission

Close the Gap

First we ship what can fight now. Fieldable, affordable systems with the people who use them, on short cycles, judged by real world performance.

Vision

The Final Weapon

Our plan starts with products in the field today. Hardware and software that operators can actually use. These systems are simple and practical for today’s command and control.

However, our individual products are really just stepping stones for a much larger master plan. A fully autonomous distributed system for defending the West, the final weapon. Achieving our vision means becoming a much larger company, growing at high speed, and staying aggressive. We are only at the beginning of this story.

About

Real Hardware, Not Renderings

Theron is a San Francisco based defense technology company, founded in 2026. We build products that exist in the field today. Every person on our team has real world skill. Mechanical design, electronics, production engineering, software, operations, and sales. We live where we work, we work extremely hard, and we measure success by what ships.
